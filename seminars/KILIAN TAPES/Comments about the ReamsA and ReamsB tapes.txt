[NOTES 1] If you are reading this, you are in the online folder holding 100+ audio files.

[NOTES 2]: Several years ago I, Rex Harrill, was getting so confused with the Reams A and Reams B audio labeling that I decided to give them each a serial number of sorts. I think Michael Olszta later improved on that. If you, dear reader, or others do any sorting, classifying, or relabeling of the audios, this would be a good place to explain your methods so someone behind you can figure things out.

Background: sometime in 2009 or 2010 or 2011 Bob Kilian's widow agreed to share two DVDs filled with old Reams audiotapes that a friend had digitized onto disks. She wanted me and other members of the RBTI Yahoogroups to have them but she couldn't do it for free.  There is much in the archives that should explain copying costs, how to order, etc. The DVDs were labeled "Reams A" and "Reams B" and many more than a hundred files were included.

May I say that the files are good stuff and terrible recordings.  However, they are what they are and I hope they fall into many more hands as time goes along. If anyone reading this decides to have transcripts created, please share them around.  You never know who might combine something from a transcript made from one of these files with another transcript from another place and have something better than either.  Things like that are very helpful to recover the wisdom of a remarkable mind. Reams has been dead for 29 years and I am absolutely convinced that the knowledge he left us is as bright and shining as ever. Treat it with respect because it could go far toward creating a much better world.
Rex Harrill April 2014
